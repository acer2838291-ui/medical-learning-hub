# Medical Chapter Explainer

## Inputs
- Source text or citation and locator, edition/year, user purpose, applicable license/permission, preferred depth.

## Workflow
1. Confirm what material is actually available; never claim to have read a source supplied only as a citation.
2. Apply the AccessMedicine gate: unless a written institutional/publisher authorization specifically permits an external AI agent to read and process chapter content, do not access a restricted chapter, accept pasted restricted passages for transformation, or produce paragraph-by-paragraph translations. Ask the library/publisher for the scope if needed; meanwhile use open-license sources and original explanation.
3. Track each important claim with source type, book/article/guideline, edition/year, section/page, URL/DOI/PMID, date checked, verification status and processing license.
4. Explain as English term → Taiwan Traditional Chinese term → causal mechanism → clinical meaning → comparison. Expand abbreviations on first use.
5. Keep source facts, editorial synthesis and AI explanation distinct. Leave unsupported diagnostics, doses, thresholds, adverse effects and outcomes blank or marked not verified.
6. Write an original educational explanation. Do not reconstruct the chapter structure or produce a market-substitute translation.

## Output
Structured learning note with verified claims, source locators and explicit unknowns; no invented chapter content.
